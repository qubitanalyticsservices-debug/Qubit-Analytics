About the course

A complete intraday strategy, taught in Hindi.

In this course you will learn a complete intraday trading strategy using price action, candlesticks, and trend analysis. It is designed for traders who want clarity, structure, and confidence in the stock or crypto markets — understanding price action behaviour, identifying trends, interpreting candlestick patterns, executing precise entries, and managing risk effectively. Instead of theory alone, the course emphasises practical examples and disciplined execution so you can confidently trade intraday setups.

Introduction

You will meet your instructor and understand the overall structure and approach of the course.

Understanding price action, candlesticks & trends

You will dive into the core concepts of candlestick basics, types, patterns, and usage. You will also learn how to analyze trends and apply them to build intraday trading strategies with high probability setups.

Risk management

You will learn how to manage risk effectively through position sizing, stop loss placement, and disciplined trade execution to achieve consistent results while protecting your capital in volatile markets.
